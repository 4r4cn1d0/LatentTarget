As I am writing this, I sit here in front of my glowing screen in a dark room with only a measly 17
years of life under my belt. I would like to think I can figure out this thing one day, but
considering my luck and habit, I won’t even get halfway. This question has bewildered everyone
and also been asked by everyone.

                       The meaning of life is quite possibly mankind’s greatest mystery. A
question so simple objectively but with an elusive answer, one that is specific to anyone asked.
Now most motivational posts on Facebook, will easily tell me the answer is to “Find the truth”.
Different religions seem to have different but somehow similar answers, which always leaves
me wondering where I fit into it.

As a naive child, I confidently thought I knew how everything worked. The television works
because pressing the button makes electricity go to the TV, and electricity was basically a blue
lightning like substance the TV “ate” very similar to how I ate my dinner. My table was made of
wood, and that was all there was to it. Why would there be any other way. Sometimes even now
I wish things could be that simple but sitting in my third grade science class and learning about
particles and molecules for the first time was something so profound and eye opening. An entire
world hidden from my eyes, too small for me to see lay out there. The fog lifted and the world
was sharper, like I had just worn glasses for the first time. “OK , now I’ve got it.” I thought, big
things are made of smaller things. Now back in the present, after starting the chapter of nuclear
physics, and calculating nuclear binding energy the old view of the world seems myopic to say
the least. According to Facebook I have found the truth but in 3rd grade I had found another
truth which had just been falsified.
